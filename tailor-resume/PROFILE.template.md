# PROFILE — copy this file to `PROFILE.md` and fill it in

Copy to `PROFILE.md` in the same folder as `SKILL.md`, then edit. `PROFILE.md` is
**gitignored** — your data never leaves your machine. The skill treats it as the source of
truth and will not invent anything that isn't in it.

## How to fill this in

**Two rules and you're fine:**

1. **Label every claim** — `[attested]` or `[keyword-depth]`.
   - `[attested]` = you could defend it in an interview and point to the repo/demo.
   - `[keyword-depth]` = it's true, but there's no backing artifact. Fine on a **skills
     line**; **never** as an experience or project bullet. A recruiter will click through.
2. **Only real numbers.** No invented metrics, no estimated-looking percentages. If you
   don't remember the exact figure, write "approx" or leave the number out entirely.

The skill will honor these labels. Anything you marked `[keyword-depth]` stays off your
experience and project bullets automatically.
